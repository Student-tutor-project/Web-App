# Student Tutor — Brand kit

## 1. Brand name
**Student Tutor**. Kept exactly as given by the founder. File slug: `student-tutor`.

## 2. Slogan
**Learn it from someone who just did.**

## 3. Positioning
A tutoring marketplace where strong students teach younger ones. Parents book a vetted student tutor for a fraction of what a professional tutor or agency charges. The tutor learned the same syllabus recently, so they explain it the way it was just taught to them. Student tutors earn a flexible side income, and the free track lets them log volunteer (VIA) hours by tutoring families who can't afford lessons.

It sits between free homework help (patchy, no one accountable) and professional tutoring (expensive). The promise is calm and plain: a good tutor, a fair price, booked in a minute.

## 4. Target audience
- **Parents** of primary and secondary school students in Singapore who want steady help with schoolwork or exams but find professional tutoring too expensive. Busy, careful with money, and protective: safety and a clear price matter more than flash. Usually booking on a phone.
- **Student tutors**: upper-secondary, JC and university students with strong grades in the subjects they teach, who want income around their timetable and VIA hours for school.

## 5. Brand personality
Trustworthy, calm, clear, warm, quietly high-tech, patient, honest.
Student Tutor feels like a well-lit study room at night: unhurried, safe, and precise, with technology that does the hard work quietly so the booking feels easy.

## 7. Typography
- **Headings:** Fraunces (Google Fonts), weights 400 and 600, soft optical size. A calm, humane serif with real character; reads as trustworthy.
- **Body and UI:** Manrope (Google Fonts), weights 400, 500, 700. Clear at small sizes on phones; used for every button and form field.

## 8. Logo direction
A wordmark "Student Tutor" in Fraunces 600, with a small mark to its left: two dots joined by a short rising arc, the left dot solid mint and the right dot an outline that the arc is just reaching. One student passing the light to the next. Works as the favicon on its own.

## 9. Product / service description
What a parent gets:
- **Matched student tutors** for primary and secondary subjects (Maths, Science, English, Chinese and more), filtered by subject, level, price and online or in person.
- **Vetted tutors:** grades checked against report cards by a person, parental consent for tutors under 18, and reviews after every lesson.
- **Clear pricing:** placeholder rates from S$20 an hour for primary and S$30 an hour for secondary (to be confirmed), shown up front with no agency fee on top.
- **Booking in three steps:** subject and level, pick a tutor, pick a time. Payment and chat stay inside the platform.
- **Safe lessons:** online, or in public places such as libraries. A report button on every lesson.
- **Free track:** families with financial need can request free lessons; tutors log those hours as VIA.

What a student tutor gets: flexible paid lessons around their timetable, a profile that shows their grades and reviews, and logged VIA hours on the free track.

## 10. Key benefits
- **Fair price:** a fraction of professional tutoring, shown up front.
- **Tutors who just learned it:** same syllabus, recent exams, explained in a way kids connect with.
- **Checked by a person:** grades verified, consent for under-18 tutors, reviews after every lesson.
- **Booked in a minute:** three steps, on any phone.
- **Safe by design:** chat and payment stay in the app; lessons online or in public places.
- **Free lessons for families who need them:** tutors earn VIA hours giving back.

## 17. Design bans
- No cream or off-white page background
- No italic accent words inside headlines
- No numbered "01 / 02 / 03" section labels
- No monospace eyebrow labels
- No pill-shaped buttons
- No gradient-filled headline text
- No emoji as icons
- No three identical feature cards in a row
- No purple-to-blue gradients
- No glass effect on every card
- No 3D, particles or heavy motion behind the booking flow or any form
- No hidden or delayed primary button: "Book a tutor" is always one tap away
- No neon overload: one glowing accent at a time
