Questions parents ask

How do you check a tutor's grades?

A person on our team compares the grades on the tutor's profile with their report card or results slip. Profiles go live only after that check.

Can a tutor be under 18?

Yes. Tutors under 18 need written consent from a parent, which we confirm before their first lesson.

Where do lessons happen?

Online, or in person in a public place such as a library or community centre. You choose when you book.

How much does it cost?

Each tutor sets an hourly rate, starting from S$20 for primary and S$30 for secondary. You see the rate before you book, and there is no agency fee.

What if something goes wrong in a lesson?

Every lesson has a report button that reaches our team. Chat and payment stay inside the platform, so you never need to share bank details.
