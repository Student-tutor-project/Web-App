How we keep lessons safe

  • Grades checked by a person

    Someone on our team reads each tutor's report card before their profile goes live.

  • Consent for tutors under 18

    If a tutor is under 18, one of their parents signs off before the first lesson.

  • Reviews after every lesson

    Parents rate each lesson, and the reviews stay on the tutor's profile.

  • Every booking on record

    You book and cancel lessons on Student Tutor, so there's a record of who taught your child and when.

  • Online, or in a public place

    In-person lessons happen in places like libraries and community centres.

    If something feels wrong, tap Report on the lesson and it comes straight to us.

Questions parents ask

How do you check a tutor's grades?

A person on our team compares the grades on the tutor's profile with their report card or results slip. Profiles go live only after that check.

Can a tutor be under 18?

Yes. Tutors under 18 need written consent from a parent, which we confirm before their first lesson.

Where do lessons happen?

Online, or in person in a public place such as a library or community centre. You choose when you book.

How much does it cost?

Each tutor sets an hourly rate, starting from S$20 for primary and S$30 for secondary. You see the rate before you book, and there is no agency fee.

What if something goes wrong in a lesson?

Every lesson has a Report button that comes straight to our team, and we'll get back to you. Lessons are booked and cancelled on Student Tutor, so there's always a record.
