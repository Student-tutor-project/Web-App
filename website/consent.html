Your child would like to tutor on Student Tutor

Student Tutor lets secondary, JC and university students give online lessons to younger students. Because the student is under 18, we need a parent or guardian to agree first.

What tutoring here involves

  • One-hour lessons by video call on Google Meet or Zoom, with Primary or Secondary school students. The student's parent books each lesson.
  • Your child chooses which hours to open and sets the hourly rate. Fees are agreed and paid between the parent and the tutor.
  • Our team checks your child's report card before any parent can see the profile. We delete the report card once the profile is approved.
  • Parents see only the first name, school year, subjects and rate.
  • Every lesson follows our lesson rules: cameras on, no swapping phone numbers or social media, and a parent nearby for younger children.
  • You can withdraw your agreement at any time by emailing hello@studenttutor.example. We then hide the profile from parents.

Read the full safety, privacy and terms page before you agree.
